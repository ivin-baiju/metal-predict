Us & Plus

A place to discover a cause and understand the people behind it. We designed and built Us & Plus around nonprofit profiles, a searchable directory and a direct path from reading about an organization to choosing a donation.

  • Product design
  • Web platform
  • Nonprofit onboarding
  • Stripe integration

Get to know the cause

Before giving, people need context. The organization’s profile brings its story, work, photographs and achievements together, with the donation action close at hand. These screens use Puentes de Esperanza, the sample organization built into the project’s public demonstration.

A small, clear next step

The donation screen keeps the organization visible while the donor chooses an amount and reviews the total. We connected that flow to Stripe Checkout and added the account-connection steps for nonprofits. The case-study captures stop before checkout; they show the interface with sample data.

A profile has work behind it

The public profile is one part of the platform. Organizations also need to register, complete their information and follow their verification status. Administrative views support review, while separate donor and nonprofit accounts keep each person’s tasks in the right place.

A shared visual language

Purple gives actions a consistent place. Mint green and warm cream make room for the stories and photographs. Nuxt and Supabase connect the directory, profiles and account workflows so the same product can serve someone exploring a cause on a phone and a team managing its information at a desk.
